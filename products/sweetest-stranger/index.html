03

Safety & directions

How to wear. Spray from 15–20 cm onto pulse points: wrists, neck and behind the ears. Let it settle on the skin; do not rub. Applied over moisturised skin, it stays longer.

  • For external use only.
  • Flammable. Keep away from heat and open flame.
  • Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse well with water.
  • Patch-test before first use; stop use if irritation occurs.
  • Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
