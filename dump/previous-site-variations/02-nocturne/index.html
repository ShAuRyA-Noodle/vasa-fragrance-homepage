03 / Wearing a fragrance

A fragrance
has its own tempo.

01

Concentration

Concentration describes one part of a fragrance’s composition. It is not a universal measure of how a fragrance will feel on every person.

02

Strength

Presence depends on the composition, the amount applied, and the setting. Choose a level that feels considered for the moment.

03

Longevity

Fragrance unfolds over time. Skin, weather, and application all influence the experience as it develops.

These are ways to understand fragrance, not performance promises.
