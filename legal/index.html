Shipping & delivery

Delivery regions, charges and timelines will be published before VASA begins accepting orders. The current website does not take payment or create a binding order.

Returns & exchanges

The returns window, eligibility rules and exchange process will be confirmed before commerce launches. Fragrance products may require intact seals and original packaging for hygiene and safety reasons.

03

Privacy

Your fragrance bag is currently saved only in your browser. VASA does not receive those selections unless a future order or enquiry is deliberately submitted.

Customer enquiries

When you contact VASA, use only the details needed to answer your request. A complete privacy notice, data-retention policy and contact address will be added before customer accounts or payments go live.

04

Terms of use

Product descriptions are editorial previews. Pricing, concentration, availability, delivery commitments and commerce terms are not final until they are published on the live store.

VASA names, marks, imagery and creative material may not be reproduced without permission.

05

Accessibility

VASA aims to make this digital experience usable with a keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion and common mobile or desktop displays. If any part of the site prevents access, please describe the page and issue through our contact form so it can be addressed.
