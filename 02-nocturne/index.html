03 / Behind the feeling

Feeling is instinct.
Fragrance is an art.

A little understanding changes the way you experience a scent. Four things worth knowing before you make it yours.

01

Concentration

+

The proportion of aromatic material shapes a fragrance’s character. Concentration is one part of the composition, alongside the balance and nature of its materials.

02

Longevity

+

A scent unfolds over time. Skin, weather and where you apply it all influence the experience. Give a fragrance time on your skin before deciding how it feels.

03

Strength

+

Presence can whisper or fill a room. The amount you apply and your surroundings help determine how closely a fragrance sits to you.

04

Family expertise

+

VASA grows from family roots in the perfume business. That shared knowledge informs our approach to fragrance: personal, considered and attentive to the details.

04 / A moment of your own

Apply to clean skin. Let it settle. Notice how it changes. Wearing fragrance is a small ritual that asks for nothing but a moment of attention.
